The project-created documentation, metadata, figure source data and result
tables are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
license (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The `frozen_inputs/` and archive files contain derived observations from two
third-party datasets. Those portions remain subject to the source terms and are
not relicensed by the project:

- ESA-ADB version 2: CC BY 3.0 IGO. Attribution to the European Space Agency
  and the benchmark contributors is required. The IGO-specific terms apply.
- Server Machine Dataset (SMD): MIT license, copyright 2021 NetManAIOps-SMD.

Where a file mixes project-created material with source-derived observations,
the source terms continue to apply to the source-derived portions. See
`NOTICE.md` for attribution, changes and the full source statements.

No raw dataset is redistributed. The derived records contain selected original
completed-window-last observations. They do not contain full windows, all
observations, below-threshold histories or fitted model objects.
