# Figure captions

**Figure 1. Complete-record storage and the distinction between first evidence and complete reports.** (a) Empirical cumulative distributions of the complete serialized archive size for 56 coarse-cadence and 56 fine-cadence SMD histories (28 devices, each evaluated with PCA and ridge). The four ESA histories are shown individually as rug marks and are not pooled into the SMD distributions. Sizes include charged archive metadata. Dashed vertical guides indicate the illustrative capacities of 64, 256 and 1,024 KiB; these are experimental capacities, not measured deployment limits. (b) Under the same 256 KiB capacity, the six static archive policies are compared on 2,329 reference-positive event/channel queries from the fine-cadence SMD histories, pooled across the two frozen score backbones. Filled circles show the fraction with a certified earliest evidence position and its exact numeric record. Open squares show the fraction with the complete exact numeric report. Each plotted rate uses the same 2,329 positive queries as its denominator. Full-if-fits retains the complete archive when it fits and otherwise uses the coverage summary with deterministic slack fill. Slight vertical offsets separate the two services within each policy. Histories, backbones and overlapping queries are not independent incident replications; the display is descriptive.

**Figure 2. Query age separates first-evidence retrieval from complete-report retrieval under online eviction.** A shared past-only replay of the fine-cadence SMD machine-3-4 ridge history compares four archive policies at a 256 KiB retained-byte capacity, with complete chunks of 1,024 evaluation cycles and first-in, first-out eviction. (a) The fraction of reference-positive queries with a certified earliest evidence position and its exact numeric record. (b) The fraction with the complete exact numeric report. The denominator in each age bin is the number of positive queries in that bin, identical across policies: 25, 37, 38, 24 and 10 from youngest to oldest (134 in total per policy). Query age is the distance from the query interval's last cycle to the latest observed cycle, divided by the number of cycles observed at the query time. Bins are 0–1%, >1–25%, >25–50%, >50–75% and >75–100%. Small horizontal offsets distinguish equal rates; connecting lines are visual guides between the ordered age groups. The two panels share a 0–1 scale. These are overlapping queries from one diagnostic history under a fixed evaluation schedule, not independent incidents or an estimate of a real user query distribution.

# Figure files and reproducibility

Each figure has a true-vector PDF with embedded TrueType fonts, an SVG with editable text, a native 1,200 dpi PNG and a separate 150 dpi PNG preview. Both fixed canvases are 183 × 110 mm. Ordinary text is Arial 7 pt; panel letters are bold Arial 8 pt. Legends sit outside the plotting data. The SVG contains no embedded raster images, and the PDF contains no image objects or Type 3 fonts. The native raster is 8,645 × 5,196 pixels, generated directly from the vector artists without interpolation.

`figure1_source_data.csv` and `figure2_source_data.csv` preserve the numerators, denominators and displayed values. Per-figure geometry reports check all text bounding boxes, text intersections with data lines and spines, clipping, font sizes, physical dimensions, vector structure and source-file hashes. `artwork_metadata.json` records the completed visual inspection separately from those deterministic checks. The 150 dpi previews were actually viewed to inspect readability and layout; exact values were checked from CSVs rather than read from the images.

The style uses the MIT-licensed [SciencePlots](https://github.com/garrettj403/SciencePlots) science/nature settings with explicit Arial, physical sizing and export controls. The previously verified reference revision is `b9b16959570bd2fbc9ff5118bacc423c3bddd592`; this is a style source, not a claim of Nature endorsement or acceptance.
