**Table 1. Complete storage under the same high record and metadata contract.** Histories are device and model or mission and model combinations and are related observations. KiB denotes 1,024 bytes. The 1 MiB column is a feasibility census of actual complete sizes.

**Table 2. Fine SMD task service at 256 KiB.** Joint first and complete report use the 2,329 positive annotated queries. Certified negative and unknown use the full 4,508 query workload, which contains 2,179 reference negative queries. All method answers are checked against the same full reference. Complete denotes an archive that stores full history or declines service; its small refusal frame is charged separately from successful compression.

**Table 3. Actual bytes and positive tasks on the constrained coarse ridge history at 256 KiB.** There are 60,483 source atoms and 20 positive annotated targets. Full if fits selects the same state as coverage+fill here and is omitted as a duplicate row.

**Table 4. Online constrained fine ridge history at 256 KiB with 1,024 decision chunks.** Positive tasks have denominator 134; support answers have denominator 176. Evictions and p95 timing cover the chronological run. All four policies use identical arrivals and queries. Query timing is warm cache wall clock time for reopening and decoding the archive in this implementation.
