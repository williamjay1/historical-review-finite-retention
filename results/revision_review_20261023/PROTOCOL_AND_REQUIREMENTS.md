# Review revision: protocol, route and requirements

Date: 23 October 2026. Retrospective redesign prompted by external critique;
not a preregistration, independent external validation, or new detector study.
Route: SCIE applied intelligent-monitoring systems; target ESWA original article.
Question: for a declared historical replay task and target range, when is complete
compression sufficient, when does narrower service preserve useful answers, and
when must the service refuse? Main contribution is evaluated service selection and
its failure boundaries; certainty/completeness theory and compression are prior art.

Inputs: original 60 frozen coarse PCA/ridge fits, available 56 finer SMD streams.
No new training, threshold search, synthetic human data, or raw download planned.
All work outputs on D. Raw sources are read-only. Labels only define/reference
evaluation queries; archive admission, extrema and fill never inspect annotations.

Before new results: compare complete reversible storage, full support+numeric
change, coverage-aware first/last/min/max/count summaries, deterministic slack
fill, and full-if-fits. All schema, framing, coverage and usability count in bytes.
Primary tasks: (1) reconstruct first high time AND exact score/window-last/version
with a certificate, (2) generate the complete requested high-record report.
Support yes/no/unknown is a diagnostic; interval witness is not task success.
Secondary tolerance: within one decision and score/value absolute tolerance
specified before analysis (0.01 in normalized score and window-last coordinates).
Not physical fault detection, causal diagnosis, analyst time, workload or safety.

Static capacities retain the previous illustrative 64/256 KiB and normalize by
complete compressed size of SAME history/record contract. No duplicated data or
shrunk budgets used to manufacture need. Descriptive evidence only, independent
device and event identity denominators; no hit/seed-based significance.

Online comparison: same chronological fixed 1024-decision chunks (including empty
chunks), target past observed history, arrival/query times and global byte ceiling.
Use actual constrained machine-3-4 ridge and ESA Mission1 PCA/ridge, plus paired
fine SMD. No future extrema; each closed chunk may be encoded only at closure.
Compare complete FIFO, bitmap+change FIFO, coverage-summary FIFO, and adaptive
full/summary with deterministic slack fill. Query generation uses only highs or
fixed historical endpoints, with delays 0, 1, 4, 16 chunks where available and
lengths 1, 64, 1024, 4096 decisions. Record age/length, expiration-boundary and
missing-chunk strata. No empirical analyst-delay distribution claimed. Rejected
chunks form unknown holes. Open buffer and transient memory are separate costs.
Full-if-fits policy uses current bytes only; offline full-fit is explicitly a
retrospective upper reference. Past-only full-size forecasts may be tested with
prefix rate extrapolation and held-future errors, without fitted test labels.

Certificate audit enumerates compatible short Boolean histories to check both
soundness and information-relative completeness. Include empty, complete negative,
partial, unavailable-input, bucket-first, and known-positive-outside-coverage cases.
Usability is a separate charged bitmap where supplied. First certification covers
strictly earlier query positions; retained positive itself need not be in a
negative-coverage range. Joint first+numeric success reported directly.

Requirements to close: R1 three service regimes/relative capacity; R2 nearest
completeness/certain-answer literature; R3 strong semantic summary; R4 full-if-fit
and fill/slack/unsaved counts; R5 executable tasks and tolerance; R6 finer stream
and upstream denominator; R7 unified online/hard case/delayed queries; R8 soundness
AND unnecessary-refusal audit/joint endpoint/usability; R9 current self-contained
SI with old notification work separately preserved; R10 all data ceilings and
negative findings retained; R11 result-driven contribution and no invented novelty;
R12 current ESWA fit, traceability and reproducible artifacts.

Stage decision: research revision CONDITIONAL until new comparisons and integration
are executed. Submission readiness will be evaluated on resulting evidence, not
on formatting, execution counts or a promised probability of acceptance.
